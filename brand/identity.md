# Visual Identity

## 01 — Identity Strategy

Presidio Web should make serious privacy infrastructure feel immediately usable without trivializing the work it performs. The identity combines an unmistakable browser frame with a confident P and a compact W-shaped processing signal: Presidio, delivered through the web. Geometry should remain calm, inspectable, and functional at developer-documentation scale as well as favicon scale.

## 02 — Logo Direction

- **Primary direction**: A constructed PW lettermark contained within a rounded browser-window symbol, paired with the Presidio Web wordmark when space permits.
- **Character**: Trustworthy, accessible, technically capable, and active.
- **Style notes**: Flat geometric construction, rounded terminals, strong negative space, and two-color maximum in normal use.
- **Avoid**: Locks, shields, fingerprints, hacker imagery, gradients, glossy effects, medical or legal symbols, and overly literal document icons.
- **References**: Borrow GitHub's ability to remain recognizable at tiny sizes, Linear's disciplined geometry and restrained color, and Mozilla's practice of giving web-native technology a human, approachable presence. Do not imitate their forms.

## 03 — Color Palette

- **Presidio Blue — `#4B83E6`**: Primary mark and interactive color; capable and clear without feeling institutional.
- **Local Teal — `#36C6AD`**: Accent for the W, active states, and local-processing signals.
- **Deep Ink — `#050B11`**: Primary dark background.
- **Cloud White — `#F7F9F9`**: Light background and high-contrast text field.
- **Palette type**: Restrained analogous cool palette.
- **Usage**: Blue owns the browser and P; teal should remain a meaningful accent rather than decorative fill. Avoid blue-on-teal text combinations.

## 04 — Typography

- **Primary**: Inter for interface, body, and wordmark exploration.
- **Secondary**: A system monospace stack for technical labels, runtime status, and code.
- **Personality**: Direct and familiar, with technical precision introduced selectively through monospace.
- **Hierarchy**: Tight, substantial sans-serif headlines; readable sans-serif body; uppercase monospace labels with restrained tracking.
- **Avoid**: Futuristic display faces, cybersecurity stencil type, and overly rounded consumer-app typography.

## 05 — Imagery

- **Aesthetic**: Product-led and architectural, showing workflows, boundaries, and results rather than generic security photography.
- **Subject matter**: Browser UI, local-processing diagrams, redaction interactions, and concrete developer integration examples.
- **Treatment**: Dark, low-noise fields with crisp blue and teal functional signals.
- **Avoid**: Hooded figures, glowing locks, server-room stock photos, legal gavels, doctors staring at tablets, or unprovable visual claims.

## 06 — Iconography & Illustration

- **Style**: Rounded geometric lines and simple filled forms derived from the logo construction.
- **Weight**: Medium to bold so icons remain legible at interface sizes.
- **Personality**: Functional first, with teal reserved for active or locally processed states.

## 07 — Design Principles

- **Show the boundary**: Make local execution, storage, and network behavior visible rather than relying on abstract privacy symbolism.
- **Reduce the ceremony**: Interfaces should make a technically sophisticated tool feel direct to operate, with advanced details available when needed.
- **Earn calmness**: Use space, restrained color, and factual proof instead of fear, urgency, or compliance theater.
- **Stay inspectable**: Prefer flat shapes, legible type, and explicit states over ornamental effects that obscure function.

## 08 — Brand Expressions

- **Website and demo**: Lead with the browser-native outcome, then expose architecture and privacy evidence.
- **Favicon and PWA icon**: Use the contained PW mark with simplified window chrome at the smallest sizes.
- **GitHub and npm**: Pair the mark with concise capability language and visible implementation proof.
- **Documentation diagrams**: Reuse blue for browser/runtime structure and teal for local activity or completed processing.
- **Presentations**: Use generous dark fields, one architectural diagram per idea, and minimal promotional decoration.
